FlipValue

AI-powered resale pricing application
Academic • Team Project

Overview

FlipValue is an application designed to help resellers and thrifters determine an appropriate resale price for an item.

FlipValue uses AI to recognize characteristics such as brands, styles, and materials, then compares the item with similar products to suggest a fair resale price.

The Problem

Determining the value of second-hand items can be difficult, especially when a reseller does not have enough information about the item's brand, style, material, or comparable listings.

The Solution

FlipValue provides users with an easier way to evaluate an item before deciding whether to purchase or resell it. The application uses AI-powered item recognition and comparisons with similar products to provide a suggested resale price.
